Two entry points to compare: Calendar toolbar (placement #3 — prototyped here for visual review) and workout card "Pace" chip (placement #2 — opens the drawer pre-seeded with the workout's sport + threshold).

The bet: a small speedometer icon in the Calendar toolbar (top-right) is one tap from the most-used surface. Tap → drawer slides up to 80% height, calculator inside is fully usable. Tap chevron / drag down / tap backdrop to dismiss.
