## Periodization principles applied

1. **3:1 build-recovery cycles** within each block (3 build weeks + 1 deload, except early/late).
2. **Volume ramps ≤10% per week** between build weeks; deload weeks drop 20-30%.
3. **Polarization target 80/<10/20 LIT/MIT/HIT** in build weeks; LIT-heavier in recovery weeks, MIT-permitted in race-specific weeks.
4. **Session complexity progresses** from single-zone (Block 1) → simple intervals (Block 2) → long intervals (Block 3) → mixed/sport-blended (Block 4) → race-rehearsal (Block 5).
5. **Strength load progresses** bodyweight → light DB → moderate load → maintenance.
6. **VO2max introduced cautiously**: short reps (2min) → medium (3-4min) → long (5-6min) → race-pace blocks.
7. **Brick frequency** ramps 0 → 1×/wk → 2×/wk in peak weeks, with one race-pace brick monthly.
8. **One full rest day per week** (Monday default). Two rest days during heaviest weeks if HRV trips a flag.
9. **Tune-up race Sep 12** is treated as a quality race-pace simulator inside the Race B taper, not as a separate peak.
10. **Heat acclimation block** in weeks 11-12 (sauna/heat-room) if Sep weather forecast >25°C.

## Heat acclimation note

Sep weather in southeast France typically 22-28°C. If the forecast 14 days out exceeds 26°C ambient, add a 10-day heat-acclimation block (weeks 11-12):

- **Sauna protocol:** 20-30 min sauna immediately post-easy-session, 4-5×/wk, hydrate aggressively
- OR **heat-room training:** indoor bike with windows closed + space heater, 30min Z2 in 28-32°C, 3-4×/wk

Avoid heat training during quality sessions — adds load without benefit.

### Workout structure (any block)

2. **Main sets (20-25 min):** 6 exercises, performed as **2 supersets** (A1+A2, B1+B2, C1+C2) — saves time
3. **Cool-down (3-5 min):** couch stretch 60s/side, pigeon 60s/side, deep breathing

### Exercise descriptions

**1. Goblet squat** *(quad/glute, primary)*
- Hold DB or KB vertically at chest, elbows tucked
- Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out
- Drop hips between heels, chest tall, knees track over toes
- Descend to thighs parallel to floor (or deeper if mobile)
- Drive through whole foot to stand
- **Form cues:** chest stays vertical, weight in mid-foot, knees don't cave
- **Substitution:** Bulgarian split squat 3×8/side if knee pain

**2. DB Romanian deadlift (RDL)** *(hamstring/glute, primary)*
- DBs in hands at thighs, feet hip-width
- Soft bend in knees, hold throughout
- Hinge from hips — push butt back, NOT down
- DBs slide down legs to mid-shin or just below knees
- Drive hips forward to stand, squeeze glutes at top
- **Form cues:** flat back, neutral neck, ribs down. If DBs leave the legs you've hinged wrong.

**3. Single-leg RDL** *(hamstring/glute + balance, primary)*
- Stand on one leg, opposite leg slightly bent behind you
- Hold DB in opposite hand (or both hands at chest for bodyweight)
- Hinge at hip — torso lowers as back leg rises (T-shape)
- Light touch with DB toward floor, then return to standing
- **Form cues:** hips stay square (no opening up), planted knee soft, back foot is a counterweight
- **Sub:** standard RDL if balance an issue

**4. Walking lunge** *(quad/glute unilateral, primary)*
- DB in each hand at sides
- Step forward 60-70cm, drop back knee toward floor
- Front shin vertical, back knee 1-2cm above floor
- Drive through front heel to come up, step into next lunge
- **Form cues:** torso vertical, don't lean over front knee
- **Sub:** reverse lunge if knee sensitive

**5. Reverse lunge** *(quad/glute unilateral, primary — default since no bench)*
- DB in each hand at sides
- Stand tall, feet hip-width
- Step ONE foot back ~70cm, drop back knee toward floor
- Front shin vertical, torso upright, back knee 1-2cm above floor
- Drive through front heel to return to standing
- Alternate sides or complete all reps one side then switch
- **Form cues:** weight stays on front leg, back leg is a kickstand, no forward lean
- **Why reverse vs forward:** less stress on front knee, easier to control descent

**5b. Bulgarian split squat (BSS) — if you have a sturdy chair / couch arm / stair**
- Stand 60cm in front of the elevated surface (40-50cm tall ideal)
- Top of back foot on the surface (laces down)
- Hold one DB at chest (goblet) or two at sides
- Drop back knee toward floor, front shin vertical
- Drive through front heel, don't push off back foot
- **Form cues:** front foot far enough forward that knee stays over ankle
- **Why it's harder:** more demand on front leg + balance, better for late-block strength gains

**6. Glute bridge** *(glute, accessory)*
- Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat 30cm from glutes
- Push through heels to lift hips until knees-hips-shoulders form a line
- Squeeze glutes hard at top, hold 1s, lower with control
- **Progression:** add DB across hips for weighted version
- **Sub:** single-leg glute bridge 3×10/side for more challenge

**7. Push-up** *(chest/shoulder/core, accessory)*
- Hands shoulder-width, body in straight line head-to-heel
- Lower chest to within fist-distance of floor
- Elbows track 45° from body (not flared 90°)
- Press up, don't let hips sag
- **Sub:** knees-down or hands-elevated (on bench) if full push-up too hard

**8. Renegade row** *(back + anti-rotation core, accessory)*
- Push-up position with DB in each hand
- Pull one DB to ribs, keeping hips square (don't rotate)
- Lower, switch sides
- **Form cues:** wide stance for more stability, slow tempo
- **Sub:** plank + single-arm DB row if too unstable

**9. Side plank** *(lateral core, accessory)*
- Lie on side, elbow under shoulder, feet stacked or staggered
- Lift hips into straight line ankle-knee-hip-shoulder
- Hold for time, breathe normally
- **Progression:** lift top leg / arm for added challenge
- **Sub:** knee-supported side plank if shoulder weak

**10. Dead bug** *(deep core, accessory)*
- Lie on back, arms vertical, knees over hips, shins parallel to floor
- Slowly extend opposite arm and leg toward floor (don't let lower back arch)
- Return with control, alternate sides
- **Form cues:** low back glued to mat. If it lifts, reduce range.

**11. Plank** *(anti-extension core, accessory)*
- Elbows under shoulders, body in straight line
- Squeeze glutes, brace abs, breathe
- Hold prescribed time
- **Progression:** shoulder taps (alternating, hips don't rotate) → plank to push-up

**12. Calf raise** *(lower leg, accessory)*
- Stand on edge of step, balls of feet on edge, heels hang
- Drop heels below step, then drive up to full plantarflexion
- 2s up, 1s pause, 3s down (slower lowering = eccentric emphasis)
- **Single-leg version:** weighted with DB in same-side hand
- **Eccentric version (Block 3+):** rise on two feet, lower on one, 3-4s descent

| Original | If you can't do | Substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet squat heavy | No 24kg+ DB | Bulgarian split squat 3×8/side @ 16kg |
| RDL | Low back issues | Glute bridge 3×12 weighted |

### Red flags — stop and consult physio

- Sharp pain (vs muscle fatigue)
- Joint pain that persists >24h post-session
- Numbness or tingling
- Bilateral imbalance >25% (one side much weaker than other)

- **Acute injury (sharp pain):** stop, ice, see physio. Don't substitute another sport without checking.
